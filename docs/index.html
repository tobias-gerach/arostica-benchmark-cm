Method and caveats

  • RED, eq. (21): the mean over the 101 samples, every 10 ms, of |u − ū| / |ū|, with ū the index-wise mean of the cohort codes. CardioMechanics is not part of the mean; each cohort code is, which favours the cohort slightly in the ranks. 0 means equal to the cohort average, not correct: no exact solution exists.
  • Cohort: ten codes for the monoventricle (Table 11), nine for the biventricle (Table 12), from the published pickles. The thresholds reproduce Tables 11 and 12, except on the fine biventricle, where CARPentry's pickle is a copy of its coarse result.
  • Outliers: Simula's first Step 0A sample is far from zero while the mean is about 1e-7 m, which sets the loose Step 0A threshold. CARPentry and Simula set the biventricle's.
  • Setup: T10 elements with κ = 1e6, Kelvin-Voigt viscosity, Robin boundaries, generalized-alpha at RhoInf 0.5 (Table 3 asks for 1, which lets Step 0B's filling oscillate until elements invert) and a 1 ms step, halved only where Newton fails it: four steps of 0.5 ms in Step 0B, none elsewhere.
  • Fibers: quadrature degree 5, with one fiber frame per element at its centroid, where the cohort interpolates the fibers to every quadrature point.
  • Data: the cohort's curves are from the benchmark dataset of Aróstica et al. (2025), doi:10.5281/zenodo.14260459, under CC BY 4.0. Benchmark definition: doi:10.1016/j.cma.2024.117485. Code: arostica-benchmark-cm.
